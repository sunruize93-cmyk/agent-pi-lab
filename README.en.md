# Agent PI Lab

**An open community exploring agent-led research with real human collaborators.**

[中文](README.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Discussions](https://github.com/sunruize93-cmyk/agent-pi-lab/discussions)

We ask whether an agent can direct a research agenda, allocate resources, guide human researchers and decide when to stop, within an agreed mission and budget. Human collaborators may use their own agents.

## Status

**Community formation, not a running autonomous lab.** No experiment, funding award or hiring commitment has been confirmed for this pilot. Repository maintenance and merges are currently human-led. We are not registering a foundation or building a new general-purpose lab platform at this stage.

## Join

- [Introduce your skills and availability](https://github.com/sunruize93-cmyk/agent-pi-lab/issues/new?template=join.yml). A doctorate is not required.
- [Propose a real research task](https://github.com/sunruize93-cmyk/agent-pi-lab/issues/new?template=task.yml), with a beneficiary, constraints and acceptance criteria.
- [Discuss resource support](docs/sponsorship.md) or start a [Discussion](https://github.com/sunruize93-cmyk/agent-pi-lab/discussions).

English contributions are welcome. Most detailed documents currently use Chinese; translations must preserve distinctions between plans, commitments and measured results.

## Proposed first pilot

An 8-week pilot, extendable to 12 weeks, will examine useful, independently reproducible experiment output from an open research-agent workflow. A real beneficiary and repository must be confirmed before launch.

Four part-time contributors will work on four paired questions under either an Agent PI or an experienced human PI with the **same AI tools and access**, yielding eight task runs. Preparation, human interventions, retries, verification and compute count toward cost. Negative findings may pass the agreed acceptance criteria.

The proposed 8-week full-cost ceiling is **CNY 112,000**, including contingency. An optional four-week extension adds **CNY 38,000**, for a **CNY 150,000** total ceiling. These are planning assumptions, not committed funding or wage offers.

See the [pilot](docs/pilot.md), [budget](docs/budget.md) and [acceptance gates](docs/acceptance.md). Four pairs are a feasibility pilot, not evidence of general superiority.

## Decision rights

After a pilot is approved, the agent may select subquestions, assign work, guide experiments, allocate bounded resources and stop or redirect a research path. Humans retain contracting, payment checks, independent validation, authorship and submission responsibilities. Substantive human research corrections must be logged and costed.

No contributor becomes an employee or accepts an obligation by opening an issue. The repository does not grant an agent general access to accounts, money or private data. See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Relationship to existing work

FutureHouse has demonstrated AI-directed scientific workflows with human experimental execution. Sakana pursues AI Scientists and recursive self-improvement. Our proposed emphasis is an open community of real researchers with auditable decision authority, costs and human interventions. We claim neither firstness nor superiority, and have no confirmed affiliation with those organizations. See [sources](docs/related-work.md).
